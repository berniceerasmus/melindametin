Presentation

The P.E.E. Method

P.E.E. is a simple structure that makes any paragraph stronger and more organised.

P — Point: State your main idea in one clear sentence.

E — Evidence: Give an example, fact, or reason to support it.

E — Explanation: Explain HOW the evidence supports your point. Link it back.

Topic: "Volleyball is beneficial for young people."

P: Volleyball is an incredibly beneficial sport for young people.

E: For example, playing in a team environment teaches communication and trust.

E: This is important because these skills are useful not only in sport, but also in everyday life at school and in friendships.

Useful linking language for P.E.E.

Point:In my opinion… / I believe… / One key benefit is… / It is clear that…
Evidence:For example… / For instance… / To illustrate… / Such as…
Explanation:This shows that… / This means that… / This is important because… / As a result…

Practice

Complete this P.E.E. paragraph with the linking phrases from the box.

Word box: for example / this shows that / in my opinion / this is important because / such as

Each sentence has a grammar or linking mistake. Write the correct version.

2. This shows that sport is very benefit for young people.

3. This is important because help students make friends.

4. For example, when students works together, they learn faster.

5. In my opinion, is important to try new things.

These three sentences form a P.E.E. paragraph but they are in the wrong order. Write them in the correct order (Point → Evidence → Explanation).

A. This means that sport teaches young people how to handle both success and failure in real life.

B. Sport teaches young people resilience and how to deal with disappointment.

C. For example, when a team loses a match, players must recover quickly and work harder for the next game.

D. This means that creative activities give people a healthy way to process their feelings.

E. Art can help young people manage stress and difficult emotions.

F. For example, drawing or painting allows students to express feelings they might not be able to put into words.

G. This shows that reading regularly builds both vocabulary and imagination.

H. Reading is one of the most valuable habits a student can develop.

I. For instance, students who read often use a wider range of words in their own writing.

J. As a result, students feel calmer and more focused when exams arrive.

K. Good time management is essential for exam success.

L. For example, a student who plans revision in advance avoids last-minute panic.

Identify whether each sentence is a Point, Evidence, or Explanation.

1. "I believe that creative hobbies are essential for wellbeing."a. Explanation
2. "For example, painting or drawing can reduce stress and anxiety."b. Point
3. "This is important because mental health is just as important as physical health."c. Evidence
4. "Sport helps young people build confidence."d. Point
5. "For instance, scoring a goal can make a player feel proud of their effort."e. Evidence
6. "This means that confidence built in sport can help in other areas of life."f. Explanation
7. "Reading regularly improves vocabulary."g. Point
8. "As a result, students communicate more clearly in their writing."h. Explanation
9. "For example, a student who reads often knows more synonyms."i. Evidence
10. "Time management is an essential life skill."j. Point

Answers: 1-b, 2-c, 3-a, 4-d, 5-e, 6-f, 7-g, 8-h, 9-i, 10-j.

Write a full P.E.E. paragraph (5–8 sentences) for ONE of these topics:

  • Option A: "Art is an important subject in school."
  • Option B: "Sport helps young people develop important life skills."

Use: a Point sentence, at least one Evidence sentence, and at least one Explanation sentence.

Answer each question with your teacher using the P.E.E. structure. Aim for 3 sentences each time.

  • Why is teamwork important in volleyball? (Point → Example → Explain)
  • How has art helped you in your life?
  • Why is it important to learn English?

Your teacher will help you build longer, more organised answers!
