Art or Sport?

Many students at secondary school have to choose between sport and art. Some people believe that sport is more important because it keeps students healthy and teaches teamwork. Others argue that art is equally valuable because it develops creative thinking and emotional expression. Personally, I feel that both subjects are essential and that schools should offer students the opportunity to do both. The reason is that a balanced education produces well-rounded young people who can work both creatively and collaboratively.

My Coach's Advice

My volleyball coach always says that the most important thing is to believe in yourself. "In my opinion," she says, "the players who improve fastest are the ones who never stop trying." I completely agree with her because I have seen it happen in my own team. Some players are naturally talented, but the ones who practise the hardest are usually the ones who perform best in competitions. My coach also believes that rest is just as important as training. She always tells us: "If you do not sleep well, your body cannot recover and you will not perform at your best." Personally, I think this is excellent advice — and I try to follow it every week.
