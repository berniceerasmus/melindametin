Why Writing Skills Matter

Many students think that writing is the least important skill in English — they prefer speaking or listening. However, strong writing skills are essential for success in education and work. Universities require students to write essays and reports. Employers look for workers who can write clear emails and professional documents. The P.E.E. method is one of the most effective ways to organise your writing because it forces you to support every opinion with evidence. Students who practise P.E.E. regularly see significant improvement in their written work within four to six weeks. In my opinion, writing is one of the skills that most strongly reflects a person's ability to think clearly and organise ideas.

Painting for Peace

Many doctors and psychologists now recommend creative activities as a way to improve mental health. Art, in particular, has been shown to reduce anxiety and help people process difficult emotions. A recent study found that people who spend just thirty minutes a week painting or drawing report significantly lower levels of stress. This is because the act of creating something focuses the mind entirely on the present moment, which gives the brain a rest from worries. In schools where art is taught regularly, students report feeling more positive about their education overall. Experts believe that if more schools prioritised creative subjects, student wellbeing would improve dramatically.
