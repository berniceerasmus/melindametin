Lesson 23: Mock Speaking Test

B1 Speaking Practice · ~45 minutes

Warm-up

🗣️ Warm-up

Think about these questions before the test. Do not write full answers — just think!

  • What grammar have you studied in the last 6 months?
  • What vocabulary topics can you talk about confidently?
  • What is one thing you want to do better in today's test?

Your teacher will time each part of the test. Try to speak without stopping and without notes!

Presentation — How the B1 Speaking Test Works

Part 1 — Personal questions (2–3 minutes): Answer questions about yourself, your hobbies, routines, and opinions. Use full sentences.

Part 2 — Describe a picture / scenario (2 minutes): Describe what you see. Use present continuous, adjectives, opinion language, and connectors.

Part 3 — Discussion (3 minutes): Discuss a topic with your teacher. Give opinions, agree, disagree politely, and explain your reasoning.

Useful language — speak confidently

Buying time:That is a good question… / Let me think for a moment…
Clarifying:Sorry, could you repeat that? / Do you mean…?
Adding:Also… / In addition… / What is more…
Contrasting:However… / Although… / On the other hand…
Concluding:Overall… / To sum up… / In conclusion…

Mock Test

Your teacher will run this section like a real speaking test. Speak aloud — do not write your answers during the test. You may use the preparation boxes to write brief notes.

Answer each question in full sentences. Give as much detail as you can.

  • Tell me about yourself. What do you enjoy doing in your free time?
  • How long have you been playing volleyball? What do you love about it?
  • What kind of art do you enjoy most — painting, drawing, or something else?
  • What was the most exciting thing that happened to you recently?
  • What are your plans for next year?

Imagine this scene and describe it in as much detail as possible:

🏐 A photograph showing a group of young athletes — around 12 to 14 years old — celebrating a volleyball championship. They are on a sports court. Some players are holding a large golden trophy. Others are jumping in the air or hugging each other. A coach is standing at the side, looking proud and happy. In the background, you can see a crowd of supporters cheering.

Use:

  • Present continuous for what people are doing
  • Adjectives to describe feelings and the atmosphere
  • Opinion language: In my opinion… / I think the atmosphere feels…
  • Connectors: In addition… / Also… / However…

Discuss this question with your teacher. Give your opinion with reasons and examples. Try to speak for at least 2 full minutes!

💬 "Should all students be required to do at least one sport and one creative activity each week?"

Think about:

  • What are the benefits of sport for young people?
  • What are the benefits of creative activities?
  • What might be the challenges?
  • Is it right for schools to make this compulsory?

After the test, answer these questions honestly:

1. What went well in your speaking test today?

2. What was the most difficult part? What will you practise more?

3. Rate your own performance today from 1–10. Explain your score.
