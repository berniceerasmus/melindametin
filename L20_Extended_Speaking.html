Presentation

The 2-Minute Monologue

At B1 level you need to be able to speak for 1–2 minutes about a topic without stopping. The secret is structure.

5-step structure:

1️⃣ Introduction (10 sec): "I am going to talk about…"

2️⃣ Main point 1 + reason (30 sec): "Firstly… because…"

3️⃣ Main point 2 + example (30 sec): "Secondly… For example…"

4️⃣ Contrast or challenge (20 sec): "Although… / However…"

5️⃣ Conclusion (10 sec): "Overall, I think… / In conclusion…"

Useful language

Starting:I am going to talk about… / Today I want to discuss…
Adding points:Firstly… / Secondly… / Also… / In addition…
Examples:For example… / For instance… / such as…
Contrast:However… / Although… / On the other hand…
Concluding:Overall… / In conclusion… / To sum up…
Buying time:That is a good question… / Let me think about that…

Practice

Complete this sample monologue with words from the box.

Word box: firstly / although / for example / overall / going to / because / however / in addition

Each sentence has a structural or grammar mistake. Write the correct version.

1. I am go to talk about my hobby.

2. Firstly, I love art because is very relaxing for me.

3. Overall, I thinks volleyball is a fantastic sport.

4. Secondly, it teach me how to work in a team.

5. Although it is difficult, I very enjoy it.

6. For example, when I is stressed, I paint.

7. In conclusion, I am go to keep practising every day.

8. However, sometimes is hard to find the motivation.

9. In addition, it help me feel more confident.

10. To sum up, art make me feel calm and happy.

These sentences are parts of a monologue. Put them in the correct order by numbering 1–5 in your notebook, then write them out in order below.

A. Overall, I think art is one of the most valuable things I do.

B. I am going to talk about why I love creating art.

C. Although some people think art is not useful, I completely disagree.

D. Firstly, art helps me express my emotions and feel calmer.

E. For example, when I am stressed, painting always makes me feel better.

F. I am going to talk about why volleyball is important to me.

G. Firstly, it keeps me fit and full of energy.

H. For example, I train four times a week with my school team.

I. Although training can be exhausting, I always feel proud afterwards.

J. Overall, volleyball has taught me discipline and teamwork.

Match each phrase to its function in a monologue.

1. "I am going to talk about…"a. Adding a second point
2. "For example…"b. Introducing the topic
3. "However…"c. Giving an example
4. "Secondly…"d. Showing contrast
5. "Overall, I think…"e. Giving a conclusion
6. "Firstly…"f. Adding the first point
7. "In addition…"g. Adding extra information
8. "Although…"h. Introducing a contrast within a sentence
9. "That is a good question…"i. Buying time to think
10. "To sum up…"j. Summarising at the end

Answers: 1-b, 2-c, 3-d, 4-a, 5-e, 6-f, 7-g, 8-h, 9-i, 10-j.
