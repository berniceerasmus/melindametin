The Young Artist Who Never Gave Up

Lena Yıldız is a fourteen-year-old girl from Izmir who has been passionate about art since she was very young. Her parents gave her a set of watercolour paints for her seventh birthday, and from that moment, she spent every spare minute creating pictures of the world around her.

When Lena was twelve, she entered a national art competition for young people. She worked for three months on her painting, which showed a volleyball match from above, full of movement and colour. However, she did not win. She felt deeply disappointed, but her art teacher told her: "If you keep painting with this level of passion, you will achieve something remarkable one day."

Two years later, at the age of fourteen, Lena entered the same competition again. This time, her painting was selected as one of the top ten in the whole country. The judges said her work showed "extraordinary emotional depth and technical skill." When she heard the news, she cried with happiness.

Lena says that art has taught her patience and resilience — two qualities she also uses on the volleyball court. "When I am frustrated on court," she says, "I remind myself of the three months I spent on that first competition painting. If I was patient then, I can be patient now."
