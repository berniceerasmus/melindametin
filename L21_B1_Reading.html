Lesson 21: B1 Reading Practice

A2+ → B1 English Lesson · ~45 minutes

Warm-up

🗣️ Warm-up

Talk about these questions with your teacher before you read:

  • Do you enjoy reading? What kind of things do you like to read?
  • Have you ever read a story about someone who overcame a big challenge?
  • What vocabulary do you think you might see in an article about sport?

Presentation

How to approach a B1 reading text

Step 1 — Skim: Read the whole text quickly in 60 seconds. Do not stop at unknown words. Get the general idea.

Step 2 — Read the questions first: Know what you are looking for before you read again.

Step 3 — Scan: Read again carefully. Underline key words and the section that answers each question.

Step 4 — Unknown words: Use context. Look at the words before and after the unknown word.

Read carefully

Volleyball Star — A True Competitor

Ana Silva has been playing volleyball since she was ten years old. She grew up in a small town in Brazil, where volleyball is one of the most popular sports. When she was fifteen, she joined her school team and quickly became the best player. Her coach was deeply impressed by her dedication and natural talent.

However, her journey was not always easy. She suffered a serious knee injury when she was sixteen, and doctors told her she might never play competitively again. She felt devastated, but she refused to give up. After eighteen months of recovery, she returned to the court — and she was better than ever.

Today, Ana plays for the national team. She says, "The most frustrating period was the injury, but it made me mentally stronger. If I hadn't gone through that experience, I wouldn't be the player I am today." Ana also paints in her free time. She says art helps her stay calm and focused before important matches.

Practice

Complete each sentence using a word from the text above.

Each sentence below about the text is incorrect. Rewrite it correctly based on the text.

1. Ana started playing volleyball when she was fifteen.

2. Ana injured her arm when she was sixteen.

3. Ana recovered in six months and returned to play.

4. Ana's coach was not impressed by her.

5. Ana gave up volleyball after her injury.

6. Doctors were sure Ana would play again.

7. Ana plays for a local team today.

8. Ana says the injury made her mentally weaker.

9. Ana grew up in a big city.

10. Ana dislikes painting.

Based on the text about Ana Silva, write True, False, or Not Given.

Match each word from the text to its meaning.

1. devastateda. the period of getting better after illness or injury
2. dedicationb. very deeply upset and shocked
3. recoveryc. compete in official matches or tournaments
4. play competitivelyd. working hard and staying committed to something
5. frustratinge. causing annoyance because you cannot change something
6. refusedf. said no to doing something, despite pressure
7. talentg. a natural ability to do something well
8. journeyh. the process of going through an experience over time
9. impressedi. having a strong positive opinion of someone's ability
10. mentallyj. relating to the mind rather than the body

Answers: 1-b, 2-d, 3-a, 4-c, 5-e, 6-f, 7-g, 8-h, 9-i, 10-j.

Answer these questions in full sentences:

1. What do you think about Ana's story? Write 4–5 sentences giving your opinion.

2. Have you ever faced a big challenge and kept going? Describe it in 3–4 sentences.

Discuss with your teacher:

  • What qualities does Ana have that made her successful? (dedication, determination…)
  • Do you know anyone in real life like Ana? What is their story?
  • What does the phrase "mentally stronger" mean? Can you give an example from your own sport?
  • Ana says art helps her stay calm. Do you use anything to help you stay calm before a match?
