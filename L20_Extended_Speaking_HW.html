Teamwork in Volleyball

Volleyball is a team sport that requires excellent communication. Each player has a specific position, and if one person does not do their job, the whole team suffers. The coach of our school team, Mrs Kaya, always says: "Your individual skills do not matter if you cannot work together." I completely agree with her because I have experienced this myself. In one match last year, I played very well individually, but our team lost because we did not communicate. After that game, we decided to work more on our teamwork. Although it took several weeks, our communication improved dramatically. In my opinion, the ability to work in a team is one of the most important skills a young person can develop — not just in sport, but in life.

How to Give a Great Presentation

Many students find it difficult to speak in front of others. However, with the right preparation, anyone can learn to speak confidently. Firstly, it is important to know your topic well. If you understand what you are talking about, you will feel more relaxed. Secondly, practise saying your speech aloud at home several times. For example, you could speak in front of a mirror or record yourself on your phone. Although this might feel embarrassing at first, it helps you hear your own mistakes. In addition, remember to speak slowly and clearly — many nervous speakers talk too fast. Overall, public speaking is a skill that improves with practice, just like volleyball or painting.
