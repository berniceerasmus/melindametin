Lesson 19: Opinion Language

A2+ → B1 English Lesson · ~45 minutes

Warm-up

🗣️ Warm-up

Talk about these questions with your teacher. Try to give a full answer — not just yes or no!

  • Do you think sport should be compulsory in school?
  • Is art more important than science? Why / why not?
  • Do you agree that practising every day is the only way to improve?

Presentation

Grammar Focus: Expressing Opinions

At B1 level, you need to give your opinion, explain why, agree, and disagree — all politely and clearly.

Giving your opinion:

In my opinion, … / I think (that) … / I believe … / Personally, I feel that … / To be honest, …

Agreeing:

I completely agree because … / That's a good point. / Exactly! / You're right — …

Disagreeing politely:

I see your point, but … / I'm not sure about that because … / I respectfully disagree — I think …

Adding a reason:

… because … / … so … / … which means … / The reason is that …

Question: Do you think sport should be compulsory in school?

In my opinion, sport should definitely be compulsory in school because it keeps students healthy and helps them focus in lessons. I also believe that team sports teach important life skills like communication and leadership. However, I think schools should offer a variety of sports so that every student can find something they enjoy.

Choose one of these questions and write a full opinion paragraph (6–8 sentences). Use at least 3 different opinion phrases and at least 2 connectors.

  • Should students have more free time at school?
  • Is it better to be good at sport or good at art?

Discuss these topics with your teacher. Use opinion language in every answer — aim for 3–4 sentences each time.

  • Should every student play a team sport? Why / why not?
  • Is it OK to disagree with your teacher or coach?
