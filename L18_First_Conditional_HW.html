Read the text and decide: True, False, or Not Given.

Elif is a 13-year-old volleyball player. She trains every Tuesday and Thursday with her school team. Her coach says that if she continues improving, she will be selected for the regional team. Elif knows that if she misses too many training sessions, she will lose her place. She has decided that if she makes the regional team, she will invite all her friends to watch the first match. Her friend Selin also plays volleyball, but Selin prefers art.

Read the short text and answer the questions in full sentences.

Playing to Win

Maya is a talented volleyball player. She plays for her school team and practises four times a week. Her dream is to play for the national team one day. Her coach always says: "If you believe in yourself, anything is possible." Maya knows that if she works hard this year, she will earn a place in the regional competition. If she performs well there, she will get noticed by national team selectors. Maya says, "If I make the national team, I will never stop thanking my coach. She has always believed in me."

1. How many times a week does Maya practise?

2. What does Maya's coach say about believing in yourself?

3. What will happen if Maya performs well at the regional competition?

4. Find one first conditional sentence in the text and underline it in your notebook.

5. Who does Maya play volleyball for?

6. What is Maya's dream?

7. What will Maya do if she makes the national team?

8. Who has always believed in Maya?

9. What must Maya do this year to earn a place in the regional competition?

10. In your own words, explain what Maya's coach means by "if you believe in yourself, anything is possible."

Rewrite each sentence as a first conditional. Keep the same meaning.

1. Maybe it will rain. In that case, we won't go.

2. She might win. In that case, she will be very happy.

3. He might not study. In that case, he will fail.

4. Maybe I will be late. In that case, don't wait for me.

5. Maybe they will lose. In that case, they will be disappointed.

6. Maybe it will be sunny. In that case, we will go to the beach.

7. Maybe she won't call. In that case, I will call her instead.

8. Maybe we will finish early. In that case, we will watch a film.

9. Maybe he will forget his kit. In that case, he can't train.

10. Maybe you will practise hard. In that case, you will improve.

Write a paragraph of 6–8 sentences called "My Big If". Imagine: If you could make one big change in your life, what would it be?
